If you own an iPhone 15 Pro, the key upgrades are the larger displays (6.3″ and 6.9″ with thinner borders), 5x optical zoom on both sizes (previously only on Pro Max), the dedicated sapphire Camera Control button, and thermal architectural improvements that reduce throttling during 4K 120fps recording. If you own an iPhone 14 Pro or older, the jump in weight reduction, battery life, USB-C 3 speeds, and Apple Intelligence performance is substantial.
Camera Control is a multi-functional flush key engineered with a tactile click switch, a capacitive force sensor for light presses, and a touch sensor for slide gestures. A light press reveals quick controls like zoom, exposure, and depth of field, while sliding across adjusts framing in real time. A full click captures a photo, and holding down records video.
